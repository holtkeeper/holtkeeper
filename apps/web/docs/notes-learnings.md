# Notes and Learnings

Part of the web app's Knowledge docs. Both are tabs of the Knowledge page (`/knowledge/notes`, `/knowledge/learnings`) and show the space picked in the spaces sidebar (`?space=`). `KnowledgePage` renders `NotesTable` and `LearningsTable` from `features/knowledge/` with `{ space, families }`, plus `teams` for `NotesTable` (the known teams, offered in the note scope editor) and `repos` for `LearningsTable` (the `forge:path` keys the space covers, ignored for _All_). The pure logic is in `lib/knowledge.ts` and `lib/learnings.ts`.

## Notes

An entry may carry an optional `match` (`KnowledgeMatch { families?, files? }`) that narrows it inside its scope: families picked from the families list and file globs relative to the repository root (`src/**/*.tsx`). `NoteEditor` edits it under the scope editor (**Only in families**, **Only for files**); rows and the note sheet show it after the title, e.g. "· family f-web · files src/**/*.tsx" (`matchParts` in `lib/knowledge.ts`). An update replaces the whole content, so saving without a match clears it.

`NotesTable` reads `GET /api/knowledge` (with `?repo=` in a repository space, where the server keeps entries covering it) and narrows to the space with `notesOfSpace`: a team lists entries naming the team; a folder lists entries whose path patterns name that folder; a family lists entries naming one of its members; a repository lists what covers it, marking entries that only reach it through a broader scope as **Inherited** (`isInherited`).

It is one card: a toolbar, a summary strip, the table and a footer.

- **Toolbar.** The status tabs **Live** (proposed and accepted), **Rejected** and **Archived** with counts over the space (`noteFilters`, `matchesFilter`); a text filter matching title, text, topics and the `kb:` id (`filterNotes`); **Kind**, **Topic** (topics from the space's notes, `noteTopics`) and **Task** filters (notes whose task kinds, `appliesTo`, name a picked task or every task); **Group** by status (the default), topic or kind. **New note** is the page header's primary action with `knowledge:write`.
- **Strip.** The space, accepted and proposed counts (the proposed count links _also in Inbox_ to `/inbox?kind=note`), notes inherited from broader scopes and accepted notes shared with other teams (`noteTotals`).
- **Table.** Columns Permalink (`kb:` and the first 8 characters of the id, `kbId`), Note (title with kind, topic, Inherited and Shared tags), Status in words with a dot, Cited (how many posted review comments cited the note, from the entry's `citations`, with the share acted on — fixed or resolved among fixed, resolved and dismissed, `citationHelpRate` — under it and `citationLine` plus the citations from similar repositories on hover; a dash when none), Author with avatar and age, and a copy-link button (`noteLink`). Rows sit under heading rows (`noteSections`): proposals first under **Waiting for review**, newest first, then the rest by status (Accepted, Rejected, Archived), by first topic (alphabetically, notes without a topic last) or by kind in `knowledgeKinds` order. In _All_, other teams' notes (by the entry's `owner`) fold into one **Other teams** row under the table.

A row opens `NoteSheet` beside the table: the URL gets `?note=<id>` (the Knowledge route keeps it next to `space`), so a link opens the same note. J and K step through the rows in table order, folded rows last; Escape closes. The header shows the short `kb:` id with a copy button for the full one (agents cite notes as `[kb:<id>]`), the title, the status, kind, scope, Inherited and subject tags, who wrote or proposed it and who edited it last. With `knowledge:write` its quiet actions are **Accept** and **Reject** for a proposal, **Archive** for an accepted note and **Restore** (accept again) for a rejected or archived one; **Edit** and **Delete** sit behind "⋯" (Delete asks first; its history goes too).

Tabs of the sheet:

- **Note** — the text as Markdown and _Where agents use it_: kind, scope, task kinds, topics, packages with ranges, who may read it (`visibilityOptions`), the permalink and **Copy link**, then _Cited in reviews_ from `GET /api/knowledge/:id/citations`: `Cited in N review comments · M resolved · K dismissed · …` (`citationLine`, only outcomes that happened), when last, how many came from similar repositories the note was not written for, and the newest citing comments on repositories the viewer may read reviews of, each with its outcome tag (`citationOutcomes`), title linking to the forge, repository, merge request, age, replies and _similar repository_ when it reached the review through the neighbours layer; the comments on other repositories are only counted. **Edit** turns the tab into `NoteEditor` (title, Markdown text with preview, and under Details kind, scope, task kinds, topics, packages, visibility, subject and evidence links); for a proposal it offers **Save and accept** and **Save as proposal**. Saves send the version they started from, so a concurrent edit answers 409.
- **History** — the current version and every previous one from `GET /api/knowledge/:id` (`noteVersions`), each with who made it, when and which fields changed from the version before it (`changedFields`); **Restore** on an older version puts its content back as a new version, guarded by the current version (`restoreInput`).
- **Evidence** — the note's links (merge requests, threads, docs), opened in a new tab.

**New note** (the header's primary action; `KnowledgePage` keeps the form state and hands it to `NotesTable`) opens the same sheet with the form, the scope prefilled from the space (`settingScopeOfSpace`); saving opens the new note.

## Learnings

`LearningsTable` reads `GET /api/learnings` (with `?repo=` in a repository space) and keeps the candidates of the repositories the space covers (`learningsInSpace`; a team covers the repositories it owns, a folder those under it). Each row carries `sourceKind` (`review-reply`, `merged`, `mcp`) and a deterministic `confidence` from 1 to 5 computed by the server from the sources alone.

- **Toolbar.** Source tabs **All**, **Review reply**, **Merged** and **Over MCP** with counts of proposed candidates (`sourceCounts`); **Section** and **Confidence** filters (Low is 1–2, Medium 3, High 4–5: `confidenceLabel`); **Sort** by evidence (replies plus a direct proposal, `evidenceOf`), confidence or newest (`filterLearnings`); **Accept N selected** when the caller may decide some row.
- **Strip.** Candidates waiting for a person (linking _also in Inbox_ to `/inbox?kind=learning`) and where they came from, plus the selection.
- **Table.** Proposed candidates only: a checkbox (only candidates the caller may decide, `selectableIds`), the rule with its repository and, for a direct proposal, who proposed it and why; the Guide section; evidence; source kind with the first merge request linked and `+N` more; confidence as five bars with the level in words (`confidenceAria` for screen readers); **Promote to**, a scope picker over the scopes the caller may edit, starting at the candidate's `targetScope` when the proposer named one, else the one `suggestedScope` points at (`suggestedScopeOf`; the scope kinds are `repo`, `family`, `path` (the repository's folder) and `team` (its first owner team), labelled _this repository_, _its family_, _its folder_ and _its team_), with `target: <scope>` under it while another scope is picked (without decide rights the cell reads `target:` or `suggested:`); and Accept, Edit (rule and section in place) and Reject. In _All_, candidates of other teams' repositories fold into an **Other teams** row.

**Accept N selected** sends `POST /api/learnings/accept` once per chosen scope with the ids picked for it. The server checks rights per candidate and per target scope, accepts one after another and writes one `learning.accept` audit event per accepted candidate. Afterwards only the failed candidates stay selected (`selectionAfter`), each with the server's reason under its rule, and a toast counts what was accepted.

Keys (outside text fields): J and K move the focused row, X toggles its selection, P accepts the selected rows or else the focused one (into its chosen scope), D rejects them. The footer shows the keys.

Under the table, **Accepted rules live in the Guide** counts accepted rules per section with links to that section of the space's Guide (`acceptedBySection`), and **Rejected** shows rejected candidates on request (they can still be accepted or edited). Rules the `learnings.autoAccept` policy accepted (`isAutoAccepted`: `decidedBy` is `AUTO_ACCEPT_ACTOR.id`, not confirmed by a person since) are behind **Show N auto-accepted** in the accepted card: each reads _accepted by Auto-accept_, starts at its accepted scope, and can be confirmed (accept again, which records the person and may move it) or rejected, which takes it out of the Guide. Reactions on review comments are not here: they are in [Quality › Reviews](reviews.md).
