# AI spend

Part of the web app's [Ops](README.md#ai-spend) docs.

## AI spend

**AI spend** (`/ops/spend`, `features/ai/`, `ai:read` or `ai:admin`; others see the 403 panel) is the Ops tab for what models cost. `AiSpendPage` checks access and renders `SpendPage`. Everything comes from `GET /api/ai/overview`, `GET /api/ai/usage` and `GET /api/ai/runs`, already limited by the server to what the viewer may read. The scope switch and the Feature, Model and Started by filters are sent to all three, so they narrow the strip, the daily chart, the breakdowns, the forecast and the runs alike; the budget card always shows the real, unnarrowed budget and caps.

- **Header.** The Ops tabs, the scope switch (_My teams_ sends the groups of My teams as `groups=` to `GET /api/ai/overview`, `/api/ai/usage` and `/api/ai/runs`, a group combination counting each of its groups; a picked group sends just that group, `viewGroups`; without home groups the body is `NoTeamsState`; nothing is requested until the scope is known, `useScope().ready`, or while `NoTeamsState` shows), a ghost **Configure** that opens Settings › AI (providers, models and the full `llm.budget` setting), a ⋯ menu with **Copy link** and **Providers and models** (Settings › AI), a **Period** switch: _This week_ (the budget week, Monday 00:00 in the instance time zone), _This month_ or _Last 30 days_ (`AiUsagePeriodKind`), with the period's start, and three single-choice filters: **Feature** (`feature=`, every feature of the table below but _Other_; the server narrows to its task kinds), **Model** (the models used in the period within the scope, most expensive first, from `GET /api/ai/usage?groupBy=model`, so picking one never hides the others) and **Started by** (`origin=`: _People_ = `interactive`, _Automatic_). The period drives the strip, the breakdowns and the runs table; the budget card and the daily chart always show the budget week and four UTC weeks.
- **Strip** (`overview.current` against `overview.comparison`): spend of the period (with the weekly limit for _This week_ while no filter but the scope is set), the week's forecast, automatic and people spend (`automaticUsd` and the rest), runs, tokens (input, output and cache), cache hit ratio, the cost change against the same elapsed span before (`periodDelta`, `formatDelta`), then errors with the error rate, runs blocked by budget and runs of unknown cost, each with a status dot.
- **Weekly budget** (`BudgetCard`, the broadest budget of `overview.budgets`: the global one or the default one). Spend of the week against `weeklyUsd` in mono with its percent, when the week resets (`untilLabel`), and a meter (`budgetBar`): spent, the forecast to week end hatched after it, and a marker with _Automatic work stops at $X_ where the reserve begins (`automaticLimit`). The status tag (`budgetState`) reads _On track_, _Close to the limit_ (spend reached `warnPercent` of the limit, or the forecast passes the automatic limit), _Automatic work stopped_ or _Limit reached_; a line under the legend says when automatic work is expected to stop or how much is left to spare at this pace. Under _Narrower budgets and caps_ (_Caps_ when there are no narrower budgets) the narrower budgets the viewer may read follow as rows with their scope, mode, status and a small bar, then two read-only cap rows from `overview.caps` (constants in code, not settings): **Write tasks** (links to Ops › Tasks; the default cap of one task, $1.50, and the most a task may ask for, $5) and **Chat replies** (links to Chat; the per-reply cap, $0.40, with the period's average cost per reply when there were replies).
- **Budget editor** (decision 14, global `settings:write`, only on the global budget). An inline form with **Weekly limit**, **Kept for people** (with the resulting automatic limit), **Warn at, %** (`warnPercent`) and **When reached**: _Block runs_ or _Warn_, the two modes `llm.budget` has. There is no "pause all" mode. `parseBudgetDraft` accepts `$` and thousands separators, rejects a limit of $0 or less, a negative reserve, a reserve above the limit and a warn share that is not a whole percent from 1 to 100. **Save budget** writes `PUT /api/settings` with key `llm.budget`, scope `{ all: true }` and the entry's id (none when only the default applies, which creates the global entry), then refreshes every AI query. The form shows who changed the entry last and when, from `GET /api/settings`.
- **Daily cost** (`DailyCost`). Four Monday-to-Sunday UTC weeks from `chartStart` (`GET /api/ai/usage?groupBy=day,origin`), narrowed by the page's filters, each day a stacked bar of people (`interactive`) over automatic spend (`originDays`), today marked in the accent. The rest of the budget week gets hatched **Forecast** bars from `overview.forecast.days` (on top of today's bar for what is left of it, alone on later days; placed by position: the first forecast day, the rest of the instance's local today, always lands on the chart's UTC today and each later one a day further, so no day is lost when the local date differs from UTC; in the hours when the instance's week has rolled over and UTC's has not, the days past the chart's last Sunday are not drawn); a future day without a forecast is a flat line. The axis top and grid come from `niceScale`; each past week shows its total under it. Below: the week's forecast, the mean automatic spend of the averaged full weeks (`overview.forecast`), last week's UTC total, and the period's spend per analyzer (`overview.topAnalyzers`).
- **Breakdowns** (`Breakdowns`, each a `GET /api/ai/usage` over the period with the page's filters): **By feature** (`groupBy=taskKind`, summed by `featureItems`), **By repository** (`groupBy=repo`, the top 8 linking to the repository hub and the rest folded into one row by `topWithRest`) and **By model** (`groupBy=model,provider`, with the provider and run count). Models whose runs have no price say so and link to Settings › AI.
- **Model runs** (`SpendRuns`, `GET /api/ai/runs?from=` the period start, paged by `before` with **Load older**). Segments filter the outcome (_All_, _Errors_, _Blocked by budget_, with the period's counts, narrowed like the runs); the header's Feature, Model and Started by filters narrow the same request. Columns: time, feature tag, repository, the run's subject with task kind and analyzer, model and provider, tokens with `tokensNote` (_N turns · X% cached_ for agents, input and output otherwise, _not sent_ for refused runs), cost (_unknown_ without a price), duration, outcome with the error, and what started it (`runStarter`: the job or analysis by its short id, `shortId`; a person by name from `AiRunList.people`, which only viewers with `users:read` get, else by the short form of their id; anything automatic by its actor).

### Features

`featureOf(taskKind)` from `@holtkeeper/ai-usage/features` (shared with the server, which turns `feature=` into the same task kinds) maps a run's task kind to the feature it is reported under:

| Feature             | Task kinds                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reviews             | `review`, `triage`                                                            |
| Agent tasks         | `write-task`, `fix`, `port`, `propagate`, `upgrade`, `propagate-fix`, `tests` |
| Chat                | `chat`, `chat-summary`                                                        |
| Guide and learnings | `guide`, `learning`, `library-digest`                                         |
| Release notes       | `release-notes`                                                               |
| Other               | everything else, e.g. `agent`, and runs without a kind                        |

A new task kind lands in _Other_ until it is added to the map. _Other_ is not offered as a filter, since it has no fixed list of kinds.

### MCP usage by person

Last on the page, under the runs and never narrowed by the filters. `McpPeople` (`features/mcp/McpPeople.tsx`, owned with Connect agents) reads `GET /api/api-surface/mcp/usage?days=7`, not the AI usage routes: MCP tool calls do not call a model. One row per person, most calls first: avatar and name, teams (the person's home groups as tags, `—` when unknown), unexpired tokens, tool calls, a 7-day sparkline (`sparkHeights`), the two most used tools, the error rate (`errorRate`) and the last call. Viewers with global `users:read` see everyone; anyone else sees only their own row, because the server sends only theirs. There is no Rate-limited column: the MCP endpoint has no request limits. **Your tokens** links to Account › Connect agents.
