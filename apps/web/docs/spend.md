# AI spend

Part of the web app's [Ops](README.md#ai-spend) docs.

## AI spend

**AI spend** (`/ops/spend`, `features/ai/`, `ai:read` or `ai:admin`; others see the 403 panel) is the Ops tab for what models cost. `AiSpendPage` checks access and renders `SpendPage`. Everything comes from `GET /api/ai/overview`, `GET /api/ai/usage` and `GET /api/ai/runs`, already limited by the server to what the viewer may read; there is no client-side scope switch.

- **Header.** The Ops tabs, the scope switch (_My teams_ sends the groups of My teams as `groups=` to `GET /api/ai/overview`, `/api/ai/usage` and `/api/ai/runs`; a group combination counts each of its groups), a ghost **Configure** that opens Settings › AI (providers, models and the full `llm.budget` setting), a ⋯ menu with **Copy link** and **Providers and models** (Settings › AI), **Started by**, **Task** (the period's top task kinds, labelled with their feature from `lib/ai-features.ts`) and **Model** (the period's top models) filters that narrow the model runs (`origin`, `taskKind`, `model`), and a **Period** switch: _This week_ (the budget week, Monday 00:00 in the instance time zone), _This month_ or _Last 30 days_ (`AiUsagePeriodKind`), with the period's start. The period drives the strip, the breakdowns and the runs table; the budget card and the daily chart always show the budget week and four UTC weeks.
- **Strip** (`overview.current` against `overview.comparison`): spend of the period (with the weekly limit for _This week_), the week's forecast, automatic and people spend (`automaticUsd` and the rest), runs, tokens (input, output and cache), cache hit ratio, the cost change against the same elapsed span before (`periodDelta`, `formatDelta`), then errors with the error rate, runs blocked by budget and runs of unknown cost, each with a status dot.
- **Weekly budget** (`BudgetCard`, the broadest budget of `overview.budgets`: the global one or the default one). Spend of the week against `weeklyUsd` in mono with its percent, when the week resets (`untilLabel`), and a meter (`budgetBar`): spent, the forecast to week end hatched after it, and a marker with _Automatic work stops at $X_ where the reserve begins (`automaticLimit`). The status tag (`budgetState`) reads _On track_, _Close to the limit_ (spend reached `warnPercent` of the limit, or the forecast passes the automatic limit), _Automatic work stopped_ or _Limit reached_; a line under the legend says when automatic work is expected to stop or how much is left to spare at this pace. Narrower budgets the viewer may read follow as rows with their scope, mode, status and a small bar.
- **Budget editor** (decision 14, global `settings:write`, only on the global budget). An inline form with **Weekly limit**, **Kept for people** (with the resulting automatic limit), **Warn at, %** (`warnPercent`) and **When reached**: _Block runs_ or _Warn_, the two modes `llm.budget` has. There is no "pause all" mode. `parseBudgetDraft` accepts `$` and thousands separators, rejects a limit of $0 or less, a negative reserve, a reserve above the limit and a warn share that is not a whole percent from 1 to 100. **Save budget** writes `PUT /api/settings` with key `llm.budget`, scope `{ all: true }` and the entry's id (none when only the default applies, which creates the global entry), then refreshes every AI query. The form shows who changed the entry last and when, from `GET /api/settings`.
- **Daily cost** (`DailyCost`). Four Monday-to-Sunday UTC weeks from `chartStart` (`GET /api/ai/usage?groupBy=day,origin`), each day a stacked bar of people (`interactive`) over automatic spend (`originDays`), future days as a flat line and today marked in the accent. The axis top and grid come from `niceScale`; each past week shows its total under it. Below: the week's forecast, the mean automatic spend of the averaged full weeks (`overview.forecast`), last week's UTC total, and the period's spend per analyzer (`overview.topAnalyzers`).
- **Breakdowns** (`Breakdowns`, each a `GET /api/ai/usage` over the period): **By feature** (`groupBy=taskKind`, summed by `featureItems`), **By repository** (`groupBy=repo`, the top 8 linking to the repository hub and the rest folded into one row by `topWithRest`) and **By model** (`groupBy=model,provider`, with the provider and run count). Models whose runs have no price say so and link to Settings › AI.
- **Model runs** (`SpendRuns`, `GET /api/ai/runs?from=` the period start, paged by `before` with **Load older**). Segments filter the outcome (_All_, _Errors_, _Blocked by budget_, with the period's counts while no toolbar filter but the scope is set); the header's Started by (_People_ = `origin=interactive`, _Automatic_), Task and Model filters narrow the same request. Columns: time, feature tag, repository, the run's subject with task kind and analyzer, model and provider, tokens with `tokensNote` (_N turns · X% cached_ for agents, input and output otherwise, _not sent_ for refused runs), cost (_unknown_ without a price), duration, outcome with the error, and what started it (`runStarter`: the job or analysis by its short id, `shortId`; a person by name from `AiRunList.people`, which only viewers with `users:read` get, else by the short form of their id; anything automatic by its actor).

### Features

`featureOf(taskKind)` in `lib/ai-features.ts` maps a run's task kind to the feature it is reported under:

| Feature             | Task kinds                                                                    |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Reviews             | `review`, `triage`                                                            |
| Agent tasks         | `write-task`, `fix`, `port`, `propagate`, `upgrade`, `propagate-fix`, `tests` |
| Chat                | `chat`, `chat-summary`                                                        |
| Guide and learnings | `guide`, `learning`, `library-digest`                                         |
| Release notes       | `release-notes`                                                               |
| Other               | everything else, e.g. `agent`, and runs without a kind                        |

A new task kind lands in _Other_ until it is added to the map.
