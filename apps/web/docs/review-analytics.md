# Review analytics

Part of the web app's [Ops](README.md#review-analytics) docs.

**Review analytics** (`/ops/reviews`, `features/ai/ReviewAnalyticsPage.tsx`, `ai:read` or `ai:admin`; others see the 403 panel) shows what merge request reviews cost and what came of them. Everything comes from one request, `GET /api/ai/reviews?period=&teams=` (`useReviewAnalytics`), already limited by the server: spend to what the viewer's `ai:read` covers, review records and comments to repositories the viewer may read analyses of.

- **Header.** The Ops tabs, the scope switch (_My teams_ sends the teams of My teams as `teams=`, a picked team just that team; without home teams the body is `NoTeamsState`; nothing is requested until the scope is known), a ⋯ menu with **Copy link**, and a **Period** switch: _This week_ (the budget week), _This month_ or _Last 30 days_, with the period's start.
- **Strip.** Review spend of the period, the mean cost per merge request with the median, merge requests with recorded spend, comments posted, replies, the accepted share of judged comments (status dot by `acceptanceTone`) and the review spend per fixed comment.
- **Cost by phase.** A stacked bar per UTC day of the period, one color per phase (`phaseInfo` in `lib/review-analytics.ts`): _First review_ (`initial`), _New commits_ (`update`), _Rechecks_ (`recheck`), _Replies_ (`reply`) and _Other_ (review runs recorded without a phase: older records and the start note's translation). Below, each phase's spend as a share bar with its run count and cost.
- **Cost per merge request.** Mean, median and 90th percentile over the merge requests whose runs recorded which one they reviewed, their count, and the 10 most expensive: title linking to the merge request (when a review record of the period names it), repository linking to its hub, the iid, a bar of its spend per phase and the cost. Spend of runs without a recorded merge request is named under the table.
- **Reviews and replies.** Full reviews (first reviews and re-reviews after a force push), reviews of new commits, skipped runs and replies; then how the bot answered when a person replied in a thread: stood by the comment (`explain`), agreed with the person (`concede`), proposed other code (`update`), or not recorded (replies stored before the answer was kept).
- **What happened to the comments.** Comments posted, agent findings the verification pass refuted before posting, comments with a suggestion and the refuted share of everything the agent raised; then the outcomes of comments on merge requests merged in the period: judged comments, accepted (fixed) and addressed (fixed or resolved) shares, threads a person replied in, 👍 and 👎, and a bar of fixed, resolved, ignored and unknown.
- **Usefulness over time.** Accepted (fixed over judged) and addressed (fixed or resolved) shares, review spend per fixed comment and comments posted; then a stacked bar per UTC day of the comments judged that day (the day their merge request merged): fixed, resolved, ignored. A link leads to Quality › Reviews, which splits outcomes by model and prompt version.
- **Noise.** How often the bot agreed with a person who pushed back (`concede` over every recorded answer, `concedeShare`), the 👎 share of judged comments, the share of reviewed merge requests that got no comment, and the mean and 90th percentile of comments per merge request with comments; then outcome bars per severity and for the 8 most commented categories, each with its judged count and accepted share.
- **Where reviews pay off.** A table of repositories by review spend: merge requests, spend, comments posted, an outcome bar, accepted share, 👎 and spend per fixed comment.
- **Speed.** For reviews and for replies apart (from finished review jobs): event to result (queued to finished, including the planned delay before start) as median and 90th percentile, queue wait (from when the job could start to a worker taking it) and work time. Below, full reviews by size class of the merge request's estimated human review time (`sizeInfo`: small under 15 minutes, medium under an hour, large under three hours, huge above) with their count, mean cost, mean duration and mean comments.

Review and comment counts read at most `REVIEWS_SCAN` review records; past that a note says they cover the newest ones only. Usage records written before phase and merge request were kept were filled in once by a store migration from their review job: the merge request from the job's options or the record's subject, `reply` for reply jobs, `update` when the merge request already had a review before the run, else `initial`. Old rechecks count toward the review they ran in. Review spend without a job stays under _Other_ and as spend without a merge request.
