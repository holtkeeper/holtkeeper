See the groups that organize your repositories, open one to read its members, stack, Guide and settings, and edit which repositories it includes. A group is a set of repositories on one forge chosen by include and exclude patterns; teams, settings, grants and Guides all attach to groups.

## What the list shows

Every group you can see: the configured groups when you may read settings for everything, plus the groups your repositories belong to. Each row shows the group's display name and key, its patterns and the health of its members. A parent group expands to show its subgroups.

A group may have a **display name**, such as _Web › Payments_, that the panel shows instead of its key everywhere: lists, the scope switch, tags in tables, Knowledge and chat. The key stays the identity: grants, settings, home groups, schedules and links always use the key.

## Change membership

Holtkeeper lists each group's repositories from the forge every 30 minutes and matches them against the patterns.

1. Open the group's **Membership** tab.
2. Edit the **include** and **exclude** patterns: exact paths like `acme/admin-ui` or globs like `acme/*-web`.
3. Before saving, the preview applies the unsaved patterns to the last listing, labelled _from the last listing_, and names who joins and who leaves.
4. Save. To pick up a project created a minute ago, press <Ui>List now</Ui> to list the forge at once.

## Work with subgroups

A subgroup splits a group one level deeper, for example a team inside a department. Its key is `<parent>.<name>`, such as `web.payments`; the parent comes from the key and never changes. A subgroup sits on its parent's forge and holds only the parent's members that its own patterns match, so anything scoped to the parent also covers it.

### Create a subgroup

1. Open the root group and its **Subgroups** tab.
2. Click <Ui>New subgroup</Ui>.
3. Enter a display name and the key under the parent.
4. Pick members from the parent's repositories as exact paths, a glob, or both.
5. Save.

On a subgroup's Membership tab the patterns pick from the parent's members: the listing panel reads **Listed from parent** and has no **List now**.

### Move a repository between subgroups

With subgroups configured, each member row on the parent has <Ui>Move to subgroup</Ui>. The target subgroup includes the path; each subgroup it leaves drops or excludes it. The Repositories tab warns about members in two sibling subgroups, because their subgroup settings would tie.

:::tip
A subgroup can be one of your home groups on its own: pick it on [Account › My teams](/guide/account/profile/#choose-your-teams).
:::
