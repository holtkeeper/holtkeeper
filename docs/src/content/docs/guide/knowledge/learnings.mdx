## Where candidates come from

- **Review reply** — someone replies to a Holtkeeper review comment with a lasting rule: "we don't do X here", "this is intentional because…", "don't flag this". Holtkeeper filters replies cheaply first (thanks, "fixed" and questions never pass), then a small AI call turns a passing reply into one imperative sentence of at most 200 characters, without personal names.
- **Merged** — candidates drawn from merged work.
- **Over MCP** — a coding agent proposed a rule with `propose_rule_change`, with a reason and sometimes a target scope. No AI call is made.

Nothing reaches agents until a person accepts it.

## Read the table

| Column     | Meaning                                                                                          |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Rule       | The candidate rule and its repository; for a direct proposal, who proposed it and why.           |
| Section    | The [Guide](/guide/knowledge/guide/) section it goes into.                                       |
| Evidence   | Replies backing it, plus one for a direct proposal.                                              |
| Source     | Review reply, Merged or Over MCP, with the first merge request linked and `+N` more.             |
| Confidence | Five bars and a level: Low (1–2), Medium (3), High (4–5). It is computed from the sources alone. |
| Promote to | The scope the rule is accepted into.                                                             |

The source tabs count proposed candidates per source. **Section** and **Confidence** filter; **Sort** orders by evidence, confidence or newest. In _All_, candidates of other teams' repositories fold into an **Other teams** row. The strip counts candidates waiting for a person (also listed in the [Inbox](/guide/home/inbox/)) and where they came from.

## Choose the scope

**Promote to** offers every scope you may edit for the candidate's repository: the repository, its family, its subgroup and team, never _All repositories_. It starts at the scope the proposer named, else at the suggested one, where a group means the repository's nearest group (a subgroup before its parent). While you pick another scope, `target:` under the picker shows your choice.

Without the right to decide, the cell only reads `suggested:` or `target:`.

:::tip
A rule taught on one repository often holds for the whole team. Promote it to the team so every repository's reviews follow it.
:::

## Accept, edit or reject

1. Tick candidates, or focus one with <Kbd>J</Kbd> / <Kbd>K</Kbd> and press <Kbd>X</Kbd> to toggle it.
2. Check **Promote to** for each.
3. Click <Ui>Accept N selected</Ui>, or press <Kbd>P</Kbd>. Without a selection, <Kbd>P</Kbd> accepts the focused row.

Rights are checked per candidate and per target scope. Candidates that could not be accepted stay selected with the reason under their rule; a toast counts what was accepted.

- <Ui>Edit</Ui> changes the rule text and section in place.
- <Ui>Reject</Ui>, or <Kbd>D</Kbd>, rejects the selected or focused candidates.

Keys work outside text fields; the footer lists them.

:::note
Checkboxes show only on candidates you may decide. Accepting writes the rule into the scope's section of the Guide, with links back to its sources.
:::

## After deciding

Under the table, **Accepted rules live in the Guide** counts accepted rules per section, each linking to that section of the space's Guide. Accepted rules sit after team rules and before policies and generated text in what agents read.

**Rejected** shows rejected candidates on request. They can still be accepted or edited; rejecting an accepted rule takes it out of the Guide.

### Rules auto-accept let through

The `learnings.autoAccept` policy can accept candidates without a person, by source (_Proposed over MCP_, _Review replies_), into the suggested scope or the repository only. Those rules are behind <Ui>Show N auto-accepted</Ui> in the accepted card, each reading _accepted by Auto-accept_.

- Accept it again to confirm it: your name is recorded, and you may move it to another scope.
- Reject it to take it out of the Guide.

<Ui>Configure</Ui> on this tab opens the review analyzer in **Settings › Analyzers**, since review
threads are where learnings come from. The auto-accept policy lives in **Settings › Policies** at
**Agent knowledge**.

## Reactions are elsewhere

👍 and 👎 on review comments are counted per finding category in [Quality › Reviews](/guide/quality/reviews/), not here. The **Learnings** tab of a review there lists the candidates drawn from that merge request.
