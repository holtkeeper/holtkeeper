## Pick the scope

The switch at the top narrows every card on Home at once.

- **My teams** shows the repositories of your home groups. Pick them on [Account › My teams](/guide/account/profile/#choose-your-teams).
- **All I can see** shows every repository your grants reach.
- **Group: Any ▾** narrows Home to one group; <Ui>Clear</Ui> goes back to _My teams_.

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Items about no repository, such as a budget warning, show in every position; an item about several repositories shows when any of them is in scope.

:::tip
If you have not picked any teams, the switch reads _My teams 0_ and a **Choose your teams** link appears. Until then _Needs attention_, _What changed_ and _Repositories_ point you there instead of showing an empty card.
:::

## Work through Needs attention

The table lists what Holtkeeper thinks a person should look at, most urgent first:

| Kind                | When it shows                                                                              |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Job failures        | An analyzer failed in the last 24 hours, grouped by analyzer and error.                    |
| Budget stops        | An AI budget stopped jobs in the last 24 hours.                                            |
| Vulnerabilities     | Critical and high findings of a repository's latest audit, with the change since the last. |
| Allowlist           | An allowlist entry you may edit expired or expires within 7 days.                          |
| Releases            | A failed read or deploy, an acceptance nobody gave, or a version blocked by a gate.        |
| Learnings and notes | Proposed rules and knowledge entries you may decide.                                       |
| Stale or missing    | The newest analysis is older than 14 days, or a repository has never been analysed.        |

Each row has one action:

- <Ui>Retry</Ui> queues the analyzer again on every repository of the item. It shows only when you
  may run analyzers on all of them; the arrow beside it opens the item.
- <Ui>Review</Ui> and <Ui>Renew</Ui> open the decision in the [Inbox](/guide/home/inbox/).
- <Ui>Open</Ui> goes to the page that explains the item.

Group the rows with **By severity**, **By kind** or **By repository** in the card's header.

### Snooze an item

Snooze a row for a day when you know about it and will get to it later. Snoozed rows are hidden and left out of the strip's count. Turn on **Snoozed N** in the card's header to see them again, tagged _Snoozed_ after the rest of their group, and unsnooze any of them there.

:::note
Snoozing is private: it hides the item for you only and is not a decision. An allowlist item shares its snooze with the Inbox.
:::

| Card               | What it shows                                                                                                                                                                            | Needs        |

| Fleet health       | One tick per repository in scope, worst first, the count per status and how many analyses are stale.                                                                                     | `repos:read` |
| AI spend this week | Spend of the broadest budget you may read against its weekly limit, split by product feature, with a marker where automatic work stops, the projection, the reserve and _Resets Monday_. | `ai:read`    |
| What changed       | One sentence per notable event of the last 7 days: versions, deploys, setting changes, accepted rules, new repositories and the like. <Ui>Full history</Ui> opens the audit log.         |              |
| Repositories       | The ten worst repositories in scope with one cell per health area you may read.                                                                                                          | `repos:read` |

In **Repositories**, a health cell opens that area of the repository hub (Security, Libraries, Coverage, Bundles, CI, Releases…) and the path opens its Overview. The footer names the scope and links to the full list when there are more.
