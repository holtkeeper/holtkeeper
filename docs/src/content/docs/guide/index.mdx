## Moving around

- <kbd>⌘K</kbd> (or <kbd>Ctrl K</kbd>) opens the [command palette](/guide/home/command-palette/):
  jump to any repository, library or page.
- Lists keep their place: a row opens as a side sheet with its id and position, and <kbd>J</kbd> / <kbd>K</kbd> step through the list.
- Filters and the open sheet live in the URL, so a copied link shows the same view to a colleague.
- **My teams** and **All I can see** switch every fleet page between your teams and everything your role can read.
