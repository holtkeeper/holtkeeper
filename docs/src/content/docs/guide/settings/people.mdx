## Find a person

The table lists everyone who can sign in with the panel's own accounts. Search matches name, email and team; **Role**, **Team**, **Sign-in** and **Status** narrow it, and the toolbar says how many of how many are shown.

| Column     | Meaning                                                                                       |
| ---------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Person     | Avatar, name, email, and `you` on your own row.                                               |
| Teams      | The person's home groups, else the groups their access names.                                 |
| Role       | Viewer, Developer, Maintainer, Admin, Custom or No access.                                    |
| Applies to | Where the role applies, or how many grants a custom set has.                                  |
| Last seen  | Their last signed-in request.                                                                 |
| Sign-in    | Passkey, single sign-on or nothing yet.                                                       |
| Status     | Active, Invited (with _Link expires in 2 days_ or _Link expired · send a new one_), Disabled. |

**Access map** draws the filtered people against what they can reach; click a person to open their sheet.

:::note
**Teams** only decide what _My teams_ shows a person across the panel. They never grant access; roles do.
:::

## Roles

Every person has one role on one scope, or an exact set of grants. The **Roles** block shows the four presets with how many enabled people hold each.

| Role       | Can                                                                                         |
| ---------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Viewer     | Read health, libraries, releases, knowledge and settings in its scope.                      |
| Developer  | Viewer, plus run analyzers and audits, start agent tasks and write knowledge notes.         |
| Maintainer | Developer, plus run release steps and edit settings in its scope.                           |
| Admin      | Everything, everywhere, including people and credentials. Always covers the whole instance. |

A scope is everything, some groups, or some repositories. A group entry can ask for repositories in two groups at once (`frontend + storefront`). Grants that match no preset show as **Custom**. See [Permissions](/reference/permissions/) for every action a grant can hold.

## Invite people

1. Click <Ui>Invite people</Ui> (needs `users:invite`).
2. Enter the name and email.
3. Pick a role and where it applies, or switch to exact grants.
4. Send. A one-time sign-in link appears **once**: copy it and hand it over yourself.

While the person has not signed in, their sheet offers <Ui>Send a new invite</Ui>, which makes a fresh link.

1. On **Role and access**, pick the new role. The current one stays marked.
2. Set **Applies to**.
3. Check **What … can do**: what stays allowed, what they gain, what they lose and what stays out of reach.
4. Click <Ui>Save</Ui>. Nothing is written before that.

<Ui>Edit exact grants</Ui> edits the raw grants instead. A custom set stays untouched unless you
pick a preset or edit it. Your own access is read only here.

The other tabs:

| Tab      | What it shows                                                                                         |
| -------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Sign-in  | Passkeys (remove one with `users:write`) and linked single sign-on identities.                        |
| Teams    | The person's home groups.                                                                             |
| Activity | Last seen, then what they did, as far as the [Audit log](/guide/settings/audit-log/) lets you see it. |

The header's <Ui>Create recovery link</Ui> (needs `users:write`) lets someone who lost their passkey back in. <Ui>Disable</Ui> and <Ui>Enable</Ui> never apply to yourself. The ⋯ menu edits name and email.

## Set the reach across teams

**Reach** is one level for every person, team and agent, without exceptions per team. It decides what people see of repositories outside their own access:

Changing it needs `settings:write` over everything. Findings, audits, jobs, releases, review output and source code never cross teams at any level.

## Make repositories read-only

**Read-only repositories** lists freezes: while a repository is frozen, Holtkeeper only analyzes it and never changes it.

1. Add an entry, pick the groups or repositories and give a reason. A new entry starts frozen.
2. Optionally set an end date; without one the freeze lasts until removed.
3. To keep one repository of a frozen group writable, add an entry for it with **frozen** switched off. The most specific entry wins.

Anyone with `settings:write` on a group or repository can lift a broader freeze there.

## Who sees this section

The people list needs `users:read`. With only `settings:read` you see Reach and read-only repositories. Inviting needs `users:invite`; disabling, recovery links and removing passkeys need `users:write`.
