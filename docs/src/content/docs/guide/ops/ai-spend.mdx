## Read the week

The page shows what agent runs cost: reviews, chat, agent tasks, the Guide and release notes. Everything is limited to the repositories you may read.

- **Period.** _This week_ is the budget week, from Monday 00:00 in the instance's time zone. _This month_ and _Last 30 days_ widen the strip, the breakdowns and the runs. The budget card and the daily chart always show the budget week.
- **Scope switch.** _My teams_ counts your home groups, a picked group just that group.
- **Feature**, **Model** and **Started by** (_People_ or _Automatic_) narrow every figure on the page but the budget card, which always shows the real budget.

The strip under the toolbar shows spend in the period (with the weekly limit on _This week_), the week's forecast, the automatic and people parts, runs, tokens, the cache hit ratio, the change against the same elapsed span before, errors with the error rate, runs blocked by the budget and runs whose cost is unknown.

## How the weekly budget works

One weekly limit covers every model run on the instance. Part of it, **kept for people**, is reserved for work a person started: a click in the panel, a chat message, a command on a merge request.

| Work                                                    | Stops when this week's spend plus the run's estimate goes above |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Automatic**: schedules, triggers, webhooks, freshness | the weekly limit minus the amount kept for people               |
| **People**: anything a person started                   | the weekly limit                                                |

With **When reached** set to _Block runs_ a run over the line is refused before the model is called, and a running agent stops when it has spent what is left. With _Warn_ the run goes on. A fresh install starts at $150 a week with $25 kept for people, blocking, warning at 80%.

The card's status reads:

| Status                 | Means                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| On track               | Spend and forecast stay under the automatic limit.                           |
| Close to the limit     | Spend reached the warning share, or the forecast passes the automatic limit. |
| Automatic work stopped | Spend reached the limit minus the reserve; only people's work runs.          |
| Limit reached          | Spend reached the weekly limit.                                              |

The meter shows spent, the forecast to week end hatched after it, and a marker at _Automatic work stops at $X_. A line below says when automatic work is expected to stop, or how much is left to spare at this pace. The forecast for the rest of the week is the mean of the same span over the last four full weeks.

:::note
When spend reaches the warning share, everyone who may change that budget gets one **Budget** item in the [Inbox](/guide/home/inbox/) for the week. There is nothing to approve: <Ui>Acknowledge</Ui> snoozes it until the week ends.
:::

## Change the budget

With `settings:write` over everything, the global budget has an inline editor under the card.

<Screen title="Ops › AI spend · Budget editor">
  <Stack>
    <Fields columns={4}>
      <Field label="Weekly limit" value={'$150'} mono />
      <Field label="Kept for people" value={'$25'} mono hint={'Automatic work stops at $125'} />
      <Field label="Warn at, %" value="80" mono />
      <Field label="When reached" value="Block runs" />
    </Fields>
    <Toolbar>
      <Tag slot="start">Changed by Mira K. · 2 weeks ago</Tag>
      <Button primary>Save budget</Button>
    </Toolbar>
  </Stack>
</Screen>

1. Enter the **Weekly limit**. `$` and thousands separators are fine; it must be above $0.
2. Enter how much is **Kept for people**: not negative and not above the limit. The form shows the automatic limit that results.
3. Set **Warn at, %**, a whole percent from 1 to 100.
4. Pick **When reached**: _Block runs_ or _Warn_. There is no mode that pauses everything.
5. Click <Ui>Save budget</Ui>. Every figure on the page refreshes.

Group and repository budgets are entries of the same setting, added in [Settings › AI](/guide/settings/ai/#budgets).
